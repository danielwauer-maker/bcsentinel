## B. Gig A – FULL ASSESSMENT (English ready-to-paste candidate)

**Title (the form auto-prefixes “I will”):** `audit your Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central data quality`

**Search tags:** `business central`; `data quality`; `microsoft dynamics`; `ERP audit`; `data assessment`

**Suggested category:** select the live Fiverr category best matching ERP / data analysis; verify actual category constraints in seller UI before publishing.

**Description (subject to final character validation in UI; target <= 1,200 characters):**

> I assess data quality in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online using BCSentinel, a structured scan and reporting workflow.
>
> You receive a prioritized Full Assessment report covering detected issues, affected-record counts, severity, relevant root-cause context and modeled business impact where supported. The report helps you decide what to investigate and improve first.
>
> For a new tenant, the initial Data Health Scan is performed once after authorized setup. If a qualifying initial scan already exists, Full Assessment unlocks that stored result instead of running another scan. Detailed access is planned for 14 days from activation.
>
> You need an authorized Business Central Online administrator to install/configure the extension. Never share credentials or API tokens in Fiverr messages.
>
> This service is an assessment, not a data-cleaning or implementation project. Estimates are not verified financial losses or guaranteed savings. Scope and price depend on analyzed record volume; please contact me on Fiverr before ordering if you are unsure of the size.

**One-line package scope:** `BC Online Data Health assessment, prioritized report, modeled impact where supported.`

**Suggested seller fields:** one revision for report presentation or explanation based on the same scan; delivery time **TBD**, chosen only after real onboarding benchmark; pricing **TBD** until commercial freeze; no automatic extra scan or removal of findings.

**FAQ (max 10; English):**

1. **Which systems are supported?** Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online only for this launch; on-premises is not included.
2. **Do I share my login?** No. Do not send passwords, MFA codes, access tokens or secrets. Your authorized administrator installs the extension and grants necessary BC permissions.
3. **Do you change any records?** No. This is a read/analysis and reporting service, not remediation or an ERP write-back engagement.
4. **Does Full Assessment run another scan?** No. It reveals an eligible initial scan; a new tenant performs its one authorized initial scan during setup.
5. **What do I receive?** A structured Full Assessment report with prioritized detected issues and relevant explanatory/impact data where supported by the checked scope.
6. **Are calculated EUR values guaranteed losses or savings?** No. These are modeled decision-support estimates; actual financial outcomes need independent verification.
7. **How long can I view my detailed results?** The proposed Full window is 14 days from activation; final timing is confirmed in the approved order scope.
8. **Can you fix the data for us?** Not in this Gig. You decide which changes to make; a separate implementation agreement would be required.
9. **How is pricing determined?** By analyzed record-volume tier. An estimate helps with scoping, but the completed authorized scan determines the authoritative count.
10. **What if I want a follow-up comparison?** Validation is a separate service after a paid Full baseline and corrective work, subject to technical comparability.

## C. Gig B – VALIDATION (English, conditional candidate)

**Title:** `validate Business Central data quality improvements after your fixes`

**Search tags:** `business central`; `data validation`; `ERP quality`; `data audit`; `microsoft dynamics`

**Description (publication blocked pending comparator E2E):**

> Have you already completed a paid BCSentinel Full Assessment and taken corrective action in Business Central Online?
>
> The Validation service provides one authorized follow-up Data Health Scan and a structured comparison against the qualifying earlier assessment. Where check definitions, scope and verified source evidence are comparable, the report can show supported changes. Where comparison is not technically reliable, the report identifies that limitation instead of claiming an issue was fixed.
>
> Validation is a follow-up service, not a first-time assessment or a data-cleaning project. It requires access to the original paid baseline and the same approved company scope. The proposed Renewal + Validation option includes 14 additional days of Full access plus one follow-up scan credit; final terms are subject to the published order.
>
> Contact me here on Fiverr before ordering if your environment, company or scan scope has changed.

**One-line scope:** `One authorized BC Online follow-up scan and an evidence-qualified comparison report.`

**FAQ:**

1. **Can I buy this as my first service?** No, a qualifying paid Full Assessment baseline is required.
2. **Do you correct our records?** No. Your team performs remediation before the follow-up scan.
3. **How many scans are included?** One Validation scan credit, once issued and used under the approved scope.
4. **Will every finding be marked Resolved or New?** No. The system reports only changes supported by comparable evidence. Ineligible checks are explicitly non-comparable.
5. **What happens if the company changes?** We must assess comparability and may need a new baseline or a different order scope.
6. **Do modeled impact changes equal realized savings?** No. They are model changes, not proof of realized financial outcome.
7. **When does access start?** Per the approved entitlement/order terms; no unpublished seven-day legacy promise.
8. **Can I receive another follow-up scan?** An additional authorized purchase is required, subject to the product scope.
9. **Can you guarantee the issues are gone?** No; validation reports observed evidence under tested methods and scoped data.
10. **What if a comparison cannot be supported?** We disclose the limitation and explain available next steps; no invented result.

Please confirm that an authorized BC administrator can install the extension. I can then confirm which Fiverr package or Custom Offer fits your scope. Please do not send credentials or confidential record exports.

**Order acknowledged, awaiting buyer requirements**

> Thank you for your order. I will review the Business Central Online scope and the submitted requirements before starting. Please complete the Fiverr order requirements and arrange for an authorized administrator to handle the extension installation. No passwords or API tokens are needed in this conversation.

**Setup instructions**

> Your administrator can install the approved BCSentinel extension in the agreed Business Central Online environment. Please complete the consent and report-language settings within the extension and register the company. I will verify the authorized scope before the first scan or any paid entitlement is enabled. Contact me here on Fiverr if you see a setup error; do not paste credentials or raw business records.

**Waiting on customer access / pause**

> The service is ready for the next authorized step, but the Business Central environment setup has not yet been completed. Please let me know through this order when your administrator has finished. If the agreed delivery timeline needs adjustment, we will handle that through Fiverr's order tools.

**Scan completed; review underway**

> The authorized Data Health Scan has completed. I am checking its technical evidence, entitlement scope and report before delivery. This message does not yet mean the report is approved; I will update the order once validation is finished.

**Full Assessment delivery (only after verified PDF and entitlement)**

> Your BCSentinel Full Assessment for the agreed Business Central Online scope is complete. The report is attached to this Fiverr delivery. It summarizes detected findings, their priorities and modeled impact where supported. The detailed access window is shown in BCSentinel and follows the approved order terms. The report is an assessment and does not certify that any actual financial losses or future savings have been realized.

**Validation delivery (only after evidence-qualified comparison)**

> Your BCSentinel Validation report is attached. It compares the authorized follow-up scan with the qualifying paid baseline **only where the evidence is comparable**. Checks that could not be reliably compared are shown as such; they have not been labeled resolved by assumption. Please review the report and contact me through this Fiverr order if a presentation correction is needed.

**Validation blocked for incomplete evidence**

> We could not produce a reliable before/after comparison for the agreed scope because some required comparison evidence is missing or not compatible. I will not report unverified improvements. I have paused the comparison deliverable and will explain the supported options here on Fiverr, including any change to the order scope or timing.

**Monitoring report delivery (blocked until operational gate passes)**

> The agreed monitoring period has completed. I have attached the approved period report, including supported trends and any documented limitations. Scan frequency and reporting rights follow your active Fiverr order and the approved monitoring terms.

**Clarification / revision**

> Thank you for the feedback. I can clarify or correct the report presentation based on the same verified scan data. A new scan, different company, additional data cleanup or wider scope is not included as a presentation revision; we can discuss a suitable follow-up offer on Fiverr.

**Price/record-volume mismatch**

> The completed authorized scan indicates a higher analyzed record-volume class than estimated before purchase. I will not unlock a higher paid scope automatically. I will confirm the correct options and any price difference with you through Fiverr before proceeding.

**Support / technical incident**

> I have identified a technical issue affecting this step. I am investigating it without asking you to share credentials or business records. I will update you within the agreed order communication window and use Fiverr's order-management options if a delivery adjustment is required.

**Close / delivery acceptance**

> Thank you for reviewing the report. If you would like a clarification within the included revision scope, please reply through this Fiverr order. Any follow-up Validation or other service requires a separately agreed order.

## G. Delivery, scope and support operating rules

1. Seller checks paid Fiverr order reference **before** operator grant; verify tenant/company and entitlement scope, one order→one intended grant; log Fiverr order ID as audit metadata only.
2. Never request BC admin login, passwords, API keys, MFA codes, raw exports, or card details. Buyer uses authorized BC admin, and the extension records technical notification email/consent.
3. No Fiverr-buyer payment redirection to Stripe, own website or off-platform invoice; service operations (extension/report delivery) are technical, not off-platform commercial solicitation.
4. One report-presentation revision is a proposed operating default, subject to final Fiverr package setup. It excludes another scan, changing records/company, remediation work, other record class or changed methodology.
5. Delivery ETA must be set after real onboarding and scan/runtime timings are measured. Do not publish an invented 24-hour SLA or guaranteed availability.
6. If technical evidence is missing, stop and report **BLOCKED**; do not deliver fabricated findings or transitions. Use Fiverr's own resolution/extension tools where needed.
7. No absolute claims such as “GDPR certified”, “zero data leaves your environment”, “guaranteed savings”, “99.99% uptime” or “all 199 checks passed” without current verified evidence. Explain data flow precisely after security/privacy signoff.
8. For reports, always distinguish modeled Estimated Loss / Potential Saving from actual Realized Saving.
9. Record-volume quote and service scope have to be approved before paid details for a larger class are opened.
10. Maintain private operator log: order reference, authorized tenant/environment/company, selected Gig, scope class, relevant scan ID, grant ID, action timestamp and reviewer. Never place sensitive tokens in Fiverr tickets.
