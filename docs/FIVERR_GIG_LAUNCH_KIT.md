## Recommended pilot packages

The package structure avoids selling Validation to a buyer who has no Assessment baseline yet.

| Package | Fiverr price | Delivery | Revision | Customer receives |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| BASIC — Data Health Check | **$79** | 3 days | 1 | Guided setup, one initial full Data Health Scan, aggregate Free Health Report, score/severity/risk-area summary |
| STANDARD — Full Assessment | **$349** | 4 days | 1 | Everything in Basic + 7-day full Assessment access, detailed findings, exact impact model, root-cause context, prioritized actions, Assessment PDF |
| PREMIUM — Assessment Plus | **$449** | 4 days | 1 | Everything in Standard + one allowed 7-day Assessment access extension for the same scan |

Why these prices: Fiverr currently pays freelancers 80% of completed order value. The Standard/Premium pilot prices preserve a materially better effective return than a low-ticket commodity audit while keeping the first controlled customers below full enterprise-consulting pricing.

### Follow-up Custom Offer

**Validation Check — $179**

Offer only after the customer completed a paid Assessment and made remediation changes.

Includes:

- exactly one new full Data Health Scan,
- before/after comparison against the paid Assessment baseline,
- resolved / improved / remaining / regressed / new findings,
- change in modeled exposure,
- Validation Report,
- 7-day full Validation result access starting with the new Validation scan.

Do not market Validation as a standalone first purchase.

## FAQ

### 1. Do you need my Business Central password?
No. Never send passwords, API tokens or user credentials through Fiverr. The BCSentinel Extension performs the scan inside your authorized Business Central environment and synchronizes the assessment result through its configured secure backend connection.

### 2. Is this for Business Central Online or on-premises?
This controlled service is designed for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online. Contact me on Fiverr before ordering if you use an on-premises environment.

### 3. What does the Basic Data Health Check include?
It includes one full BCSentinel Data Health Scan and an aggregate Health Report with Data Health Score, issue/severity counts, risk areas and impact band. Detailed findings and exact modeled financial values are part of the paid Assessment package.

### 4. What does the Full Assessment add?
It unlocks the existing scan for 7 days and provides detailed findings, affected counts, modeled Estimated Loss / Potential Saving, root-cause context, prioritized actions and the professional Assessment Report.

### 5. Will the Assessment run a second scan?
No. Assessment normally unlocks the existing initial scan. A new scan is used later for Validation after remediation.

### 6. What is the Validation Check?
Validation is a follow-up service after a paid Assessment. Once you have implemented fixes, I enable one new full scan and BCSentinel compares it with the Assessment baseline.

### 7. Are the financial values guaranteed savings?
No. Estimated Loss and Potential Saving are modeled decision-support values based on detected data/process conditions and documented methodology. They are not accounting-confirmed or guaranteed savings.

### 8. How long can I access detailed results?
Assessment and Validation provide 7 days of full result access for their qualifying result window. Downloaded reports remain yours. The stored scan is not deleted when live access expires.

### 9. Can you assess more than one company or a very large tenant?
Possibly. Send the scope on Fiverr first. Large/multi-company environments may require a Custom Offer.

### 10. Is ongoing monitoring available?
BCSentinel supports a Monitoring product, but the first Fiverr controlled pilot focuses on Data Health Check, Assessment and follow-up Validation. Ask on Fiverr if ongoing monitoring is relevant.

## Revision policy

Use **1 revision** per package to satisfy package setup while protecting scope.

Revision means correction of the delivered summary/report presentation or clarification of the analysis based on the same scan data. It does **not** include a new scan, changed company scope, changed source data or remediation work. A new post-remediation scan is Validation.
