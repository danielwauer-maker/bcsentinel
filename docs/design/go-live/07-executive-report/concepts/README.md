# Executive Summary Report — 10 explored concepts

1. Executive First — management summary and decision first.
2. Financial Impact First — business value and estimated loss/saving first.
3. Risk Matrix — risk vs. impact prioritisation.
4. Decision Memo — situation, impact, recommendation, decision.
5. Two-Page Story — minimal two-page narrative, especially suitable for Free.
6. Scorecard — KPI/scorecard-led executive layout.
7. Action Plan — finding-to-action workflow as the report narrative.
8. Outcome Tracking — realised savings and improvement over time.
9. Monitoring Delta — changes since the previous report and re-validation.
10. Print First — A4/grayscale-safe, low-ink layout.

## Selected final combination
The final design combines 1 + 2 + 5 + 7 + 8 + 9 + 10:
- Executive-first hierarchy.
- Financial Impact as a signature element.
- Free limited to two pages and aggregate-only content.
- Assessment includes full findings and concrete measures.
- Monitoring adds deltas, trend, realised savings and validation.
- HTML is the primary digital format; PDF is a print-safe derivative.
