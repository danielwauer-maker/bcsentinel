# LP-GL-08 — Security, Trust and Legal

## Scope

- Replaced the generic homepage security block with an evidence-based enterprise section.
- Described the Business Central → backend → access decision → product experience flow.
- Limited claims to architecture currently represented in the repository.
- Added direct links to Security, Privacy, Legal Notice, Terms, and Contact.
- Removed unsupported certification, partnership, and guaranteed-protection language.

## Verified claims represented

- Tenant, environment, and company context are evaluated together.
- Server-side access snapshots control protected capabilities.
- Access snapshots use a short cache TTL.
- Product modes receive different capabilities and access periods.
- Scan execution uses explicit lifecycle states and hardened execution controls.

## Claims intentionally not made

- No unsupported ISO, SOC, TÜV, Microsoft partnership, or compliance badge.
- No absolute data-security guarantee.
- No claim that the public website itself constitutes a legal or security assessment.
