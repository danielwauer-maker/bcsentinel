## 1. Home / Landing page

**SEO title:** BCSentinel | Data Health and Business Impact for Business Central Online  
**Meta description:** Identify, prioritize and better understand data-quality risks and their potential business impact in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online. Controlled pilot.

**Eyebrow:** DATA HEALTH · BUSINESS IMPACT · BUSINESS CENTRAL ONLINE  
**H1:** Poor data costs time. BCSentinel makes risks visible.  
**Lead:** BCSentinel analyzes data quality in your Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online environment and turns technical anomalies into prioritized decision support – from risk indicators to modeled business impact.  
**Primary CTA:** Request a free initial scan [only if registration/pilot flow is approved]  
**Secondary CTA:** How BCSentinel works  
**Scope:** Designed exclusively for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online (cloud); on-premises support is not advertised.  
**Pilot notice:** BCSentinel is being prepared for a controlled pilot. General availability has not been approved.

### Benefit cards
- **Spot problems earlier:** Data-health checks flag suspicious patterns and affected areas.
- **Understand business relevance:** Severity, affected volume and modeled impact indicators provide context.
- **Prioritize next steps:** Focus on the most important findings and review actions.
- **Check improvement:** A separate Validation Scan can compare changes against an eligible Assessment baseline, once that workflow is approved.

### How it works
1. **Install and register:** Install the standard extension in a supported Business Central Online environment and register the tenant.
2. **Run the initial scan:** One full Data Health Scan executes enabled checks; the free entry provides exactly one initial scan.
3. **Review the results:** Free shows aggregate risk signals; paid access layers may unlock more details of the same scan.
4. **Take action in Business Central:** Recommendations support human review; BCSentinel does not autonomously correct ERP records.
5. **Validate changes if eligible:** A later Validation Scan can assess changes within a defined comparable scope.

### Executive Report
**Title:** Turn findings into priorities.  
**Copy:** Clear reports summarize data-health status, risk areas, severity and suggested next steps. Full Assessments may add modeled financial risk and potential savings. Such figures are estimates, not verified losses or guaranteed savings.  
**CTA:** Explore sample reports [approved synthetic examples only]

### Home FAQ
**Does BCSentinel replace my BC consultant?** No. It supports review and prioritization; business decisions and corrections remain with your team or consultant.  
**Does it fix data automatically?** No autonomous remediation is performed.  
**Can it scan on-premises Business Central?** The current intended scope is Business Central Online only.  
**Are financial amounts actual savings?** No. Estimated Loss and Potential Saving are modeled; a rescan does not alone prove money saved.  
**Is BCSentinel generally available?** No. A paid pilot requires separate technical, legal and operational approval.

## 4. Security & Trust

**H1:** Data health starts with trust.  
**Lead:** ERP analysis requires traceable permissions, controlled data flows and explicit responsibilities. Security properties must not be presented as guaranteed without matching technical and contractual evidence.

- **Environment:** Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Online.
- **Permissions:** Only approved roles required for selected scan and report functionality; real non-SUPER testing remains a release gate.
- **Isolation:** Tenant and company separation require real A/B negative tests prior to unconditional claims.
- **Transport and storage:** Confirm encryption, hosting, retention, sub-processors and controls against current infrastructure evidence and DPA/TOM.
- **Data movement:** Do not state that data never leaves the customer's environment. Relevant scan data or evaluations may be transmitted to or processed by the BCSentinel service; disclose the actual flow.
- **No autonomous changes:** ERP record correction remains under authorized Business Central users.
- **No unverified badges:** Do not claim ISO/SOC certification, Microsoft AppSource availability or GDPR compliance without documented evidence.

**CTA:** Ask a security or privacy question
